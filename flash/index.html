Flash the Tribin sensor

Put the live sensor code on your XIAO ESP32-C3 from this browser, connect it to Wi-Fi, and watch it on the dashboard.

1Wire the sensor (USB unplugged)

A02YYUW sensor wireXIAO pin
Red (power)3V3
Black (ground)GND
White (TX, data out)D7
Yellow (RX)Not connected

2Install the code

  1. Plug the XIAO into this computer with a data USB-C cable.
  2. Click Connect below, pick "USB JTAG/serial debug unit" from the list, and click Connect.
  3. Click Install Tribin Live Sensor, then Install. It takes about a minute.

This browser can't talk to USB devices. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer.

This page needs a secure connection (https) to use USB.

If the XIAO doesn't appear in the list: unplug it, hold the B button, plug it back in, let go, and try again. After installing in this mode, press the R button once to start the code.

3Connect it to Wi-Fi

  1. Right after installing, the page asks to connect to Wi-Fi. If it doesn't, click Connect again, pick the XIAO, and choose Change Wi-Fi.
  2. Pick your network and type the password. A phone hotspot works best; campus Wi-Fi usually blocks devices like this.
  3. The XIAO only works on 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. On an iPhone hotspot, turn on Maximize Compatibility.
  4. The board saves the Wi-Fi, so next time just plug it in.

Your Wi-Fi password is sent straight from this page to the board over USB and saved only on the board.

4Watch it on the dashboard

  1. Open the dashboard, sign in, and click All bins. Bin 3 (Test Bin) is first, with a green LIVE tag.
  2. Its Landfill bar follows the real sensor, updating every few seconds.
  3. Sign in as the admin, open Settings → Live sensor, point the sensor at your empty container, and click Use current reading as empty. Now 0% is empty and the bar fills as you add things.

Not showing up? Click Connect here, pick the XIAO, and open Logs & Console. Each line shows the distance, the Wi-Fi address, and whether it's sending to the dashboard.
