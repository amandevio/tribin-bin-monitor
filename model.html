Tribin sensor system

Outdoor tri-bin with fill sensors · tap to expand

  1. Each compartment has an ultrasonic sensor under the top, pointing down.
  2. Every 15 minutes the controller wakes, switches the sensors on, and measures the distance to the trash.
  3. It turns that into a fill percentage and sends it over Wi-Fi to the app.
  4. Then it switches the sensors off and sleeps to save battery.
